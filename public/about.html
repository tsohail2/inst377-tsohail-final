About CyberSide

The graph speaks for itself...

Within the last few years cyber attacks have been on a sharp rise, once information systems were seen as not only an exploitable resource, but also a means for organizations to function, criminals had a new target.

CyberSide was created as a minimal way to defend people against that through a way to check what you are visitng. Utilizing Googles Safe Browsing API, we are able to use their advanced threat database to check your links.

You upload your link, we send it to Google to check it out for you. Once we send your link it gets checked, and then the important info is sent back for us and we can give you an accurate way to proceed
